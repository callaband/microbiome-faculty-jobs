[Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International License (CC BY 4.0)](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/).

Please credit "Microbiome Faculty Jobs" with a link to this repository.

The job postings themselves belong to the hiring institutions and the job boards that publish them. This project keeps only short excerpts needed to identify and classify a posting, and links to the original. Always rely on the original posting for eligibility, dates and application instructions.
